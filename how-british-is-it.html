Look at the flag

"Why does empire keep appearing — even in things that seem entirely domestic?"

Look at the items where the gap between your rating and the historical record was largest. That gap is not a mistake in your reasoning. It reflects something the historian Sathnam Sanghera calls imperial amnesia — a structural forgetting, not a personal one.

The question worth sitting with is not whether empire shaped these things, but why that shaping is so hard to see from inside British culture.

Now look at the flag behind this page differently. The Union Jack is not one flag. It is three overlapping flags — St George's Cross (England), St Andrew's Cross (Scotland), St Patrick's Cross (Ireland) — each representing a different political and military history, layered into a single symbol. The result looks natural. It was constructed.

The next instrument will ask: who made that invisibility possible?
